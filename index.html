Mission brief
Experiment 001

Goal: Provide at least 100% of a population's annual calories using autonomous farming.

Win condition: Reach the calorie target without water, energy or robot labor constraints.

Model warning: Illustrative numbers, not field-validated forecasts. One-crop calories ≠ complete nutrition. Costs exclude land, water rights, transport, housing and healthcare.
